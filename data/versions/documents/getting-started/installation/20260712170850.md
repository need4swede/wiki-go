# Installation

Neptune runs on Apple TV, iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Install it on as many devices as you like; your account, preferences, and watch state carry across all of them.

## Requirements

| Device | Minimum |
|--------|---------|
| **Apple TV** | Apple TV 4K (2nd generation or later), tvOS 18.5 or later |
| **iPhone / iPad** | iOS or iPadOS 16 or later |
| **Mac** | TBD |
| **Jellyfin Server** | 10.10 or later |

## TestFlight Installation

Neptune is currently in **beta** and available through TestFlight on every platform. One invite covers all your devices. Access is managed through the Neptune Discord server.

### Getting Access

1. Join the <a href="https://discord.gg/HTuf4HPMbP" target="_blank">Neptune Discord</a> server
2. Complete verification on the landing page
3. Head to the **TestFlight** channel and press **Board the TestFlight** to receive your Passenger role
4. Open the **Aviation School** channel and follow the instructions to access the TestFlight link

### Installing

Accept the TestFlight invitation once, then install Neptune from the TestFlight app on each device:

- **iPhone / iPad:** open the TestFlight link, accept, and install Neptune directly from TestFlight
- **Apple TV:** install **TestFlight** from the tvOS App Store, sign in with the same Apple Account, and Neptune appears under your available apps

### Tester Roles

Neptune has two tester tiers within the community:

**Passengers** have access to public TestFlight builds. These releases are less frequent but more stable, intended for general users following along with development. All verified Discord members qualify as Passengers.

**Pilots** have access to the private TestFlight with nightly-style builds featuring more experimental changes. Pilot status is earned through active community participation, constructive discussion, and well-documented bug reports submitted through Aviation School.
