# Top Shelf Customization

Top Shelf brings Neptune onto the Apple TV Home Screen before you open the app. Its free foundation can surface your library in Rows or Banners; Neptune Pro unlocks the deeper presentation and behavior controls that turn it into a tailored gallery.

This feature applies to Apple TV. iPhone and iPad do not have the tvOS Top Shelf surface.



## Pro Presentation

| Feature | What It Changes |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Cinematic style** | A full-bleed carousel with rich title presentation |
| **Neptune Studio designs** | Uses server-rendered artwork designs for Banners or Cinematic surfaces when a compatible Studio plugin is available |
| **Media badges** | Adds 4K, HDR, Dolby Vision, and Atmos flags to Cinematic details |
| **Cast credits** | Adds starring and director lines to Cinematic details |



## Pro Content and Behavior

| Feature | What It Changes |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Section ordering** | Reorder Continue Watching, Next Up, Recently Added, Favorites, Genre Spotlight, Hidden Gems, and Music Videos |
| **Items per section** | Choose 5, 10, 15, or 20 titles instead of the Free default of 10 |
| **Play action** | Selecting a Top Shelf item can begin playback immediately instead of opening its detail page |

Play/Pause on the remote always starts playback regardless of the click-action preference.



## What Remains Free

Free users can enable Top Shelf, use Rows or Banners, choose standard artwork behavior, show or hide sections, hide watched items, and show Continue Watching progress. Pro adds Cinematic presentation, richer details, Studio design selection, ordering, expanded item limits, and direct Play behavior.



## Configuration

Open **Settings > User Preferences > Appearance > Home > Top Shelf**. Presentation controls choose the style and artwork; Content & Behavior controls sections, item limits, watched filtering, and click behavior.

If Pro access ends, Neptune falls back to the Free style, default section order, ten items per section, standard artwork, and Open Details behavior. Saved Pro choices remain available if Pro is restored.
