# Downloads

Neptune Pro lets you save movies and episodes to the current device and play them without a connection to your media server.



## Download Options

| Option | Best For | Result |
|--------|----------|--------|
| **Original quality** | Maximum quality and retaining embedded tracks | Downloads the source file without re-encoding |
| **Transcoded quality** | Saving device storage or reducing transfer size | Asks the backend to create a smaller copy at the selected profile |

Neptune includes High, Medium, and Low transcode profiles by default, while the [Neptune Transcoder](/plugins/transcoder) plugin grants a server administrator the ability to replace that ladder and decide whether Original quality is offered.



## The Offline Experience

Downloads are more than saved files. Neptune keeps the metadata needed to present and play them coherently offline:

- Movie and series artwork
- Episode and season organization
- Playback position and duration
- The tracks actually present in the downloaded file
- Content restrictions for the active profile

Completed media appears in the Home screen's [Downloads row](/browsing/home-screen/downloads) and the device-wide **Settings > Downloads** manager. Active transfers show progress and estimated time remaining.



## Device Storage

Downloads stay on the device where they were created; they are not synced as media files to other Neptune clients. Remove individual items from their context menu or manage the full offline library under **Settings > Downloads**.

Original files retain their embedded audio and subtitle tracks. The initial space-saving transcode path creates one audio track and no selectable subtitle tracks, and converts HDR according to the backend's tone-mapping configuration.



## If Pro Ends

Neptune pauses active transfers, stops active server transcodes, and hides offline browsing and playback. It retains downloaded files, snapshots, progress, and queued records on the device rather than deleting them.

When Pro returns, the offline library becomes available again. Transfers remain paused until you explicitly choose **Resume**; a transcoded transfer may restart its encode from the beginning.
