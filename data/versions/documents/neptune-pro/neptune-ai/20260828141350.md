# Neptune AI with Pro

Neptune Pro includes Neptune AI, the bespoke first-party intelligence system developed alongside Neptune's features.
It is ready without an API key, local server, endpoint, or model selection.

Neptune AI currently powers natural-language Search, Episode Finder, Title Insights, and Person Insights, with a first-party foundation that can expand into other parts of Neptune.

## What Pro Unlocks

Pro unlocks the hosted **Neptune AI provider**, not the idea of AI features themselves.
Free users can connect Ollama, Gemini, or OpenRouter, but they supply and maintain the model, compute, endpoint, or API account.

Neptune AI adds:

- A model system trained and tuned specifically for Neptune's media workflows and structured feature behavior
- First-party integration with Neptune's grounding, verification, confidence, and native result presentation
- Neptune's intelligent query cache for common and closely related searches
- Immediate availability across supported Neptune devices after Pro is restored

## Integrated by Design

Neptune AI and the app can evolve as one system.
A feature can introduce a purpose-built grounding source, validation step, or structured response rather than asking a generic chatbot to improvise an answer.
Neptune AI is developed alongside the core app, making the pair work better than any third-party provider ever could.

Bring-your-own providers remain useful for local-only inference or direct model control.
They can stay enabled as fallbacks behind Neptune AI—or lead the priority list with Neptune AI ready when a local machine is offline.
