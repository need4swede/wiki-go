# Playback Menu

The in-player menu for everything about the current stream. Swipe **down** on the remote during playback (or press the **Playback Menu** action button) and a glass panel slides in from the top of the screen with five tabs.

| Tab | What's Inside |
|-----|---------------|
| **Audio** | Track selection, sound presets, Night Mode, A/V sync |
| **Subtitles** | Track selection, style, timing, position |
| **Video** | Quality, zoom, de-interlacing |
| **Chapters** | Jump to a chapter |
| **Info** | Codec, bitrate, and stream details |

Moving around is simple: left/right switches tabs from anywhere, up/down moves through the rows, Select applies, and Back steps out of a drill-in before closing the menu. The Chapters tab hides itself when an item has no chapters.

You can reorder or hide tabs in **Settings > Playback > Controls > Playback Menu**.



## Audio

**Track** lists every audio track with its codec and channel layout ("TrueHD Atmos • 7.1"). Switching happens in place without restarting the video stream.

**Preset** applies an EQ profile: **Default**, **Bass** for more low end, or **Voice** for clearer dialogue.

**Night Mode** tames loud music and effects so late-night viewing doesn't wake the house. Set it to **On**, or **Auto** to engage on a schedule (10:00 PM to 7:00 AM by default, adjustable in Settings).

**A/V Sync** opens a live adjuster: the menu gets out of the way and you nudge audio earlier or later in 20 ms steps while watching, up to half a second in either direction. The setting persists for future playback.

Hold Select on any row for a **Reset to Default** option.



## Subtitles

**Track** lists subtitle tracks with **Forced**, **SDH**, and **External** badges, plus an Off entry.

**Style** adjusts appearance without leaving playback: font size, text color, background style, and brightness. The same options live in [Subtitle Style](/settings/subtitles) settings.

**Timing** opens a live offset adjuster (up to 5 seconds either way in 0.1 s steps) for subtitle files that don't quite line up. The offset applies to the current file only.

**Position** raises or lowers subtitles with a guide line showing where they'll rest. Handy for keeping captions out of burned-in credits or letterbox bars.



## Video

**Quality** switches between Direct Play and the bitrate ladder (120 Mbps down to 1 Mbps). Each option says whether it means direct playing or transcoding for the current file. Changing quality restarts the stream in place: same position, same tracks, no kick-out to the menu.

**Zoom** picks **Fit** (the whole frame, with bars if needed) or **Fill** (fill the screen, cropping the edges). Applies instantly.

**De-interlacing** controls combing removal for interlaced sources like DVDs and TV broadcasts: **Auto** engages when frames are flagged interlaced, **On** forces it, **Off** never de-interlaces. Available on the Trident engine.



## Chapters

A scrolling list of chapters with thumbnails, names, and timestamps, opened at the chapter you're currently in. Selecting one seeks straight there and closes the menu.



## Info

A live technical readout of the current stream: engine, container, video codec and dynamic range (down to the Dolby Vision profile), audio codec and channels, subtitle details, whether the server is direct playing or transcoding, buffer health, and file details. The same data is available as an on-screen card via the **Technical Info** action button if you want it visible while watching.
