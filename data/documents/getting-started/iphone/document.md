# Neptune for iPhone

Neptune has an iPhone companion app, currently in beta. It connects to the same Jellyfin server, shares your account, and syncs your preferences with your Apple TV when the [Neptune MDM](/plugins/mdm) plugin is installed.

## Installing

The iPhone beta is distributed through the same TestFlight program as the Apple TV app. Follow the steps in [Installation](/getting-started/installation) to get access, then install Neptune from TestFlight on your iPhone.

## What's There

The iPhone app is built for the phone rather than scaled down from the TV version:

- Portrait-first design with touch controls throughout
- Home, library browsing, and item details
- Instant search, including AI search
- Discover and requests through Seerr
- Playback through Trident, with the same format support as the TV app
- An episode scroll wheel for skimming long seasons quickly

## Beta Notes

The iPhone app is newer than the Apple TV app and doesn't cover every feature yet. Parity is closing with each build. If something you rely on is missing, the fastest way to flag it is through the Discord.
