# Installation

## Requirements

| Requirement | Minimum |
|-------------|---------|
| **Device** | Apple TV HD or Apple TV 4K |
| **OS** | 18.5 or later |
| **Jellyfin Server** | 10.10.x or later |

## TestFlight Installation

Neptune is currently in **beta**. Those who are eligable will receive an invite link to join the TestFlight.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Install **TestFlight** onto your device via the App Store |
| 2 | Open your invite link from your iPhone, iPad, or Mac |
| 3 | Accept the invitation |
| 4 | Open **TestFlight** app on your device |
| 5 | Select **Neptune** and tap **Install** |

## App Store Installation

When Neptune exits beta, it will be available to download via the App Store.

### Steps

1. Open the **App Store** on your device
2. Search for **"Neptune"** or **"Neptune Player"**
3. Select **Get** to download and install
4. Open Neptune to launch it

## After Installation

Once installed, Neptune will guide you through the initial setup.
See [Setup](/getting-started/setup) for detailed instructions.
