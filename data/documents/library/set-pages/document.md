# Set Pages

Every decade, year, genre, mood, person, studio, and network in your library has its own page.
Cards on one page open other pages, so you can start anywhere and keep going.

A page only shows rows your server can fill.
A row with too few titles hides itself.

## Ways In

| From | What Opens |
|------|------------|
| **Library > Wander** | A decade, year, person, studio, network, genre, or mood |
| **Library > Browse** | Era, genre, mood, person, studio, and network pages from their cards and directories |
| **Moods** on the Library page | A mood page |
| **Explore** on a movie or show page | See below |
| **Pins** | The page for a pinned genre, studio, network, person, or tag |

## Decades and Years

A decade page opens with the decade in large numerals and a bar for each year.
On Apple TV, press Up from the buttons to move through the years and Down to return.
Focus a year to see how many titles it has.
On iPhone and iPad, drag along the strip or tap a bar to open a year.
Drag the ruler beside the numerals to slide to another decade or year in place, so Back returns to where you came from.

| Row | What It Shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **Greatest Hits: Movies** and **Greatest Hits: TV** | The best-rated titles of the decade |
| **Critics' Picks** | Titles critics rated highest |
| **Best Year of the 1990s** | The decade's strongest year, with that year's titles |
| **Year by Year** | One poster per year, so you can jump in |
| **Faces of the 1990s** | People who appear in several of its titles |
| **Still Unwatched** | Titles from the decade you haven't seen |
| **Turning 30 This Year** | Titles whose release anniversary falls this year |
| **Studios of the 1990s**, **Moods of the 1990s**, **Genres of the 1990s** | Cards that open those pages |

Turning This Year appears only when a year in the decade is a round age this year.

## Genres and Moods

| Row | What It Shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **Greatest Hits** | The best-rated titles in the genre or mood |
| **Critics' Picks** | Titles critics rated highest |
| **Hidden Gems** | Unwatched titles that people rate highly but few have rated |
| **Comedy by Decade** | The genre or mood through the decades |
| **Faces** | People who appear in many of its titles |
| **Pairs Well With** | The genres that share the most titles with this one |
| **Recently Added** | The newest titles |

See [Ratings](/browsing/item-details/ratings) for where the scores come from.
Rows built on ratings need Neptune's ratings to have reached your library, and hide until they do.

## People

A person's page gains rows that look across your library.

- **Shuffle** and **See All**
- **Greatest Hits** and **Not Seen Yet**
- **Career by Decade**
- **Frequent Collaborators** and **Studios They Work With**
- **Range**, which lists the genres they appear in
- **First and Latest**

## Studios and Networks

A studio or network page has its own rows for your best titles from the company, what you haven't watched, the cast, directors, and writers who return, and the genres it does best.
See [Studio and Network Pages](/library/studio-network-pages) for the full page.

## See All

**See All** opens every title in the set as a poster grid.
Posters have no captions, and the focused title shows a preview with its logo, year, runtime, rating, and overview.
On iPhone and iPad, sections follow the sort, and a long press previews a title with Play and Pin.

**Sort & Filter** has these choices:

| Choice | Options |
|--------|---------|
| **Sort** | Rating, Release Date, Title, Date Added, Runtime. Select a sort again to flip it |
| **Order** | Ascending or Descending |
| **Show** | All, Movies, or Shows |
| **Watched** | Show only watched or unwatched titles |
| **Year** | A year inside a decade, or a decade inside anything else |

Eras, genres, and moods start on **Rating**.
People, studios, and networks start with the newest first.
Other sets start on **Title**.
On Apple TV, the choices apply when you select **Done** or press Back.
On iPhone and iPad, they apply as you choose, and **Clear Filters** appears when nothing matches.

## Surprise Me and Shuffle

Apple TV set pages open with **Browse** selected, followed by a dice button for **Surprise Me**.
On iPhone and iPad, Surprise Me and Shuffle sit in the page header, and their order follows your Interface Stance.
Surprise Me picks from the set and follows the same filters as [Library Surprise Me](/library#surprise-me).
**Shuffle** opens a random unwatched title from the set.

## The Trail

On Apple TV, pages you open from set pages stack up as a trail.
Back returns one page at a time, and Back from the first page closes the trail.
A title you open from a set page opens on the same trail, so Back returns you to the set.
The trail keeps up to 12 pages and drops the oldest after the first when you go further.

## Explore on a Movie or Show Page

The last section of a movie or show page is **Explore**.
It links to the title's year, its decade, up to three of its genres, and its first studio or network.
When your library has more to offer, it also links to **More from** the title's filmmaker, and to the genre narrowed to the title's decade, such as **Comedy from the 1990s**.

On iPhone and iPad, Explore does not appear on titles that exist only in Discover, or on downloaded copies.
