# Library

The Library page shows what you have and helps you find something to watch tonight.
It opens on a wall of your own posters, with counts, your libraries, moods, collections, and ways into every decade, genre, person, and studio.

## The Shelf

The top of the page drifts through a wall of posters from your library.
Over it sit a headline and a row of counts.

- A sentence such as **38 titles arrived this week**.
  With nothing new, it tells you how many titles are unwatched instead.
- **Movies**, **Shows**, **Episodes**, and **Unwatched** counts.
  A count your server can't supply is left out.
- **Overview**, **Browse**, **Surprise Me**, and **Edit** buttons.

On Apple TV, Surprise Me is the dice button and Edit is the pencil.
The poster wall pauses while the header is off screen and holds still when Reduce Motion is on.

Below the header, the Shelf has these sections in default order:

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Your Libraries** | One card per library with fanned posters and how many titles are new this week |
| **Moods** | Your library by tone, built from [Smart Tags](/library/smart-tags) |
| **Collections** | Box sets with fanned posters and a film count |
| **Wander** | New ways into your library on every visit |
| **Still on the Shelf** | Titles you never watched that were added more than three months ago |
| **Browse by** | Artwork rows for Genres, Studios, Networks, and People, plus links to Tags, Format, and Specifications |

A section stays out of the page when your server can't answer it.
Moods, for example, needs Smart Tags.

On iPhone, Your Libraries is a swipeable row of cards with page dots.
On iPad it scrolls sideways.
On Apple TV, long-press a library card to rearrange it.

### Wander

Wander is a row of doors into your library.
Each visit offers a decade, a year from another decade, a person, a studio or network, a genre, and a mood.
Each door opens its [set page](/library/set-pages).

### Edit Library

Select **Edit** to reorder the sections or hide the ones you don't use.
Browse by moves and hides as one section.
**Restore Default** puts everything back.

On iPhone and iPad, drag the handles to reorder and use the switches to hide.
Your choices sync with your other devices through [Settings Sync](/plugins/mdm/settings-sync).

The full grid, Browse by Era, and Library Stats are under **Browse** and **Overview**.
Pins are on Home, as described in [Pins](/library/shortcuts).

## Overview

**Overview** summarizes what has arrived lately.
It starts with the last seven days.
If nothing was added, it widens to 30 days, then to 12 months.
The subtitle names the window it is using, and an empty year shows an empty state.

| Tile | What It Shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Recently Added** | The newest arrival and the latest posters, grouped by the day they were added |
| **Quality** | The resolutions of those arrivals, as bars |
| **Movies**, **Shows**, **Collections**, **Episodes** | How many arrived in the window |
| **Unwatched Movies & Shows** | How many of the new movies and shows you haven't watched |

Select a count to open the matching titles.
Select **Quality** to compare your whole library.

## Browse

Each way of looking at your library has its own page.

| Page | What You Browse |
|------|-----------------|
| **All** | The full poster grid with filters and sorting (iPhone and iPad) |
| **Collections** | Your box sets, newest first |
| **Moods** | Your library by tone |
| **Eras** | Your library by decade |
| **People** | Actors, directors, and writers |
| **Genres** | Every genre your library covers |
| **Studios & Networks** | The companies behind your titles |
| **Quality** | Resolution, dynamic range, audio, containers, and codecs |
| **Tags** | [Smart Tags](/library/smart-tags): themes, settings, and moods |

On iPhone and iPad, these are a row of chips under the Library header, and **Browse** opens All.
On Apple TV, **Overview** opens a panel with a rail of these pages on the left, and **Browse** opens the [full grid](#full-library-grid).
A page appears only when your server and account can supply it.

People, Genres, Studios, and Networks start with artwork cards above a searchable directory.
You can sort by name or by title count.
Select a card to open its [set page](/library/set-pages).
**See All** opens the whole directory.

### Eras

On Apple TV, Eras is a skyline with one column per decade between your oldest and newest titles.
Focus a decade to see its counts and best titles below it.
Select it to open the [decade page](/library/set-pages#decades-and-years).

On iPhone and iPad, Eras is a poster grid grouped by year.
Drag the scrubber at the edge of the screen to jump to a year.
It ticks as you pass each decade and shows the year and how many titles it has.

### Quality

**Quality** compares your whole library by what the files contain.

| Choice | Pages |
|--------|-------|
| **Specifications** | Resolution, Dynamic Range, Audio, and Subtitles |
| **Format** | Containers and Video Codecs |

Select a value to browse the movies and shows that match.
Counts come from a scan of your library.
**Refresh** and **Full Rescan** appear if your technical data looks out of date.
Installing the [Neptune Indexers](/plugins/indexers) plugin makes these views instant.

## Surprise Me

**Surprise Me** picks something for you to watch.
It shows one title with its synopsis and cast, and when it would end if you started now.

| Filter | Choices |
|--------|---------|
| **Kind** | Movies, Shows, or both |
| **Length** | Under 100 min, 100 to 140 min, or Over 140 min |
| **Mood** | Any mood, or one of your moods |
| **Genre** | Any genre, including broad ones such as Animation |
| **Unwatched** | Only titles you haven't seen |
| **Ends Before** | Only movies that finish before a time you choose |

Select **Spin Again** for another pick, or **Play** to start it.
On iPhone and iPad, swipe the top card away for the next one, or tap **Details**.
If nothing fits, Neptune says so and you can turn a filter off.

Surprise Me also appears on [set pages](/library/set-pages), where it picks from that decade, genre, mood, or person.
It is separate from the Surprise Me slot machine in [Smart Tags](/library/smart-tags#surprise-me).

## Full Library Grid

**Browse** on Apple TV opens the full grid: a collapsible filter sidebar on the left, a poster grid on the right, with infinite scroll.
The sidebar expands when you navigate to it and collapses to icons while you browse.
On iPhone and iPad, **All** is the same grid with filters in a sheet.

### Media Types

Pills at the top switch content type: **All**, **Movies**, **Shows**, **Collections**, **Favorites**.
The Collections view adds its own pills for movie, show, and mixed collections.

### Filters

| Filter | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Watched** | All, Unwatched, Watched, or In Progress |
| **Libraries** | Limit results to specific libraries |
| **Genres** | Multi-select, with an Exact Match toggle for items that must have every selected genre |
| **Year** | Presets like Last 5 Years, each decade back to the 1920s, Classics, or a custom range |
| **Rating** | Minimum community rating (4+ up to 9+) |
| **Finish Time** | Apple TV only. Find movies that end at a time you pick |

**Reset All Filters** clears everything; a badge shows how many filters are active.

**Finish Time** works out when a movie would end if you started it now.
**Ends At** matches movies that finish during the minute you choose.
**Ends By** includes everything that finishes earlier too.
For a movie you have started, Neptune uses the time that remains.
A clock time that has already passed means that time tomorrow.

### Sorting

Sort by **Name**, **Date Added**, **Release Date**, **Community Rating**, **Critic Rating**, **Runtime**, or **Random**, ascending or descending.
Random is a good way to settle a "what do we watch" argument.

A TV Shows library, or the Shows filter, also offers **Date Episode Added**.
It orders shows by when each one last gained an episode, so a show with a new episode moves to the top.
It appears only when the grid holds shows alone.

## Navigation Tips

- **Swipe left** from the grid to reach the sidebar, **swipe right** to return
- On Apple TV, press Up from the topmost control to open Compass
- **Refresh** re-syncs library data from your server
- The grid lazy-loads, so even very large libraries scroll smoothly
- On Apple TV, **Back** follows the pages you opened: from a year inside a decade, one press returns to the decade and the next returns to Library
