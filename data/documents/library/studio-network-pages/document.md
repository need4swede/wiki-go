# Studio and Network Pages

Every studio and every network has its own page.
It shows what you own from the company and, with Seerr connected, what you could request.

Open one from **Library > Browse**, from a Pin, or from a movie or show page.
The page looks the same on Apple TV, iPhone, and iPad.

## Studios

A studio page is built around films.

| Section | What It Shows |
|---------|---------------|
| **Header** | A rotating set of the studio's backdrops, its logo or name, and counts for **In Library**, **Watched**, **Avg Rating**, and **Coming Soon** |
| **Signature Films** | The studio's best-rated films, owned or not, marked **In Library** or **Request**. Without Seerr this becomes **Highest Rated in Your Library** |
| **Through the Decades** | The studio's filmography by decade |
| **Coming Soon** | The studio's upcoming releases, soonest first |

The header has **Shuffle**, which plays a random unwatched film you own, plus **Pin**, **About**, and **See All**.
**About** shows facts about the company.

Through the Decades shows films you own in full color and films you don't own as gray posters.
Move through the decades to see how many films the studio made and how many you have.
On iPhone and iPad, drag sideways through the timeline.
It opens on the decade you own the most of.
Select a decade to open its films as a grid.

## Networks

A network page is built around shows.

| Section | What It Shows |
|---------|---------------|
| **Header** | The network's newest episode among your shows, or its newest show, with counts for **Shows**, **Episodes**, **Returning**, and **Ended** |
| **This Week** | Episodes of your shows airing this week, by day |
| **Your Shows** | Your shows from the network with a status of **New Season**, **Returning**, or **Ended**, and your progress such as "4 episodes left", "Caught up", or "Not started" |
| **Originals You Don't Have** | Popular shows from the network that aren't in your library |
| **Returning Soon** | Announced seasons, with dates |

An **On This Week** badge appears when one of your shows airs in the next seven days.
The header's main button is **Continue** with the next episode, such as **S3 E2**.
It also has **Shuffle**, **Pin**, **About**, and **See All**.

## More Rows

Below these sections, both kinds of page add rows from your library:

- **Still Unwatched**
- **Cast**, **Directors**, and **Writers** who work with the company most often
- The genres the company does best

A director or cast member opens their own page, and Back returns you to the company.
See [Set Pages](/library/set-pages) for how these pages connect.

**See All** opens every title you own from the company as a poster grid with **Sort & Filter**.

## Logos and Seerr

The page works without Neptune Pro and without Seerr.
Without Seerr, it shows your library only.
The company logo needs [Neptune Pro](/neptune-pro/tmdb) with TMDB Metadata on.
Without it, the page shows the company name in a designed wordmark.
