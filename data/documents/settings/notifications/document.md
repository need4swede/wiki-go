# Notification Settings

Configure in-app notifications that keep you informed about new content, updates, and server status.



## Master Toggle

**In-App Notifications** - Enable or disable all notifications. When off, no notifications of any type will appear. Individual category toggles are only available when the master toggle is on.



## Notification Categories

Each category can be independently enabled or disabled:

| Category | What It Notifies |
|----------|-----------------|
| **New Library Content** | When new movies or series are added to your library |
| **Series Updates** | When new episodes are available for shows you're watching |
| **Request Updates** | When media requests are approved or declined (requires Jellyseerr) |
| **Server Status** | When the server goes offline or comes back online |



## Behavior

- Notifications appear briefly at the top of the screen
- Each notification auto-dismisses after a few seconds
- Duplicate notifications are suppressed within a short window to avoid clutter
- Selecting a notification navigates to the relevant content
- Notifications are paused during video playback so they don't interrupt your viewing
