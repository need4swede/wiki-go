# Built-in Themes

Neptune includes ten built-in themes. Ocean is the default.

## Ocean

Deep blue underwater aesthetic with animated effects.

- **Accent**: Teal/cyan

## Midnight

Rich purples and deep blues.

- **Accent**: Purple/violet

## Mint

Fresh teal and purple combination.

- **Accent**: Mint green

## Void

True black optimized for OLED displays.

- **Accent**: Subtle gray

## Nordic

Cool grays and icy blues.

- **Accent**: Ice blue

## Volcano

Warm reds and oranges.

- **Accent**: Orange/red

## Nebula

Cosmic pinks and oranges with a deep violet base.

- **Accent**: Pink/magenta

## Forest

Vivid green glow over a deep forest canvas.

- **Accent**: Bright leaf green

## Ember

Warm gold on deep charcoal.

- **Accent**: Amber/gold

## Twilight

Bright evening blue over a deep navy background.

- **Accent**: Evening blue
