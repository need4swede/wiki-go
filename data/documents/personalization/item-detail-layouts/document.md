# Item Detail Layouts

Choose what appears on movie and TV show pages.
Apple TV has separate Movie Pages and Show Pages customization.
iPhone and iPad keep the Detailed, Immersive, and Minimal layouts.

Open **Settings > Account > Appearance** to find the options for your device.

## Apple TV

In Appearance, open **Layouts**, then **Movie Pages > Customize** or **Show Pages > Customize**.
The editor shows a sample page that changes as you turn its options on and off.

| Option | Movies | Shows |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Logo** | Logo artwork or title header | Logo artwork or title header |
| **Poster** | Movie poster | Show poster |
| **Description** | Synopsis above the controls | Synopsis above the controls |
| **Credits** | Director and Starring | Director and Starring |
| **Ratings** | Community and critic ratings | Community and critic ratings |
| **Video** | Picture details | |
| **Audio** | Sound details | |
| **Size** | File size | |
| **Seasons** | | Show's season count |
| **Episodes** | | Show's episode count |

Movie and show choices are independent.
Turning off the hero's Description leaves the full description available in Overview.
Turning off Credits leaves the Cast & Crew section available below.

**Save** applies the draft.
**Back / Menu** leaves without saving it.
Managed options stay read-only.

Below the hero, sections flow down the page with native focus scrolling.
Shows have an inline season poster and episode gallery; movies can have an inline Collections gallery.
See [TV Shows](/browsing/item-details/tv-shows), [Collections](/browsing/collections), and [Extras](/browsing/item-details/extras) for their controls.

## The Layouts

On iPhone and iPad, **Appearance > Item Detail Page** offers three layouts:

| Layout | Best For |
|--------|----------|
| **Detailed** | A poster and a fuller view of the title's information |
| **Immersive** | A large artwork header with details in a sheet beneath it |
| **Minimal** | A simpler artwork-led page with the title, facts, and playback controls |

They use the same media, artwork, and playback actions.
iPad adapts the columns and artwork to its available space, including landscape and multitasking.

## Choosing a Layout

On Apple TV, use Movie Pages or Show Pages to customize the page's components.
On iPhone and iPad, choose a layout in **Settings > Account > Appearance > Item Detail Page**.
Changes apply to the supported pages on that device.

## Description Toggles

On iPhone and iPad, Minimal offers separate choices for showing the item description and episode descriptions.
Use those to trim the visible text while retaining playback and other actions.
Episode spoiler protection has its own controls under Appearance.
See [Cards & Backdrops](/personalization/cards-and-backdrops#spoiler-controls).

## Immersive Layout Details

On iPhone and iPad, Immersive puts the artwork first and lets you scroll into the information beneath it.
Long descriptions can be opened for the full text.
Backdrop framing and manual positioning work across the touch layouts.
See [Cards & Backdrops](/personalization/cards-and-backdrops).

## Big Picture Mode

Apple TV retains a **Detail Sections** choice of Rows or Pages for supported detail views.
The current movie and show pages use their own continuous section layout.
Their Movie Pages and Show Pages options control what the hero shows.

## Display Size

**Settings > Account > Appearance > Layouts > Display Size** offers Small, Medium, Large, and Extra Large on Apple TV.
It changes card and text sizes on supported browsing surfaces, including Home, Library, and Search.
Medium is the default.

## Cross-Platform Sync

With [Settings Sync](/plugins/mdm/settings-sync), layout preferences follow your account.
Each platform uses the options its interface supports.
A [Device Override](/settings/device-overrides) can keep an eligible choice different on one device.
Administrators can manage supported options through [Neptune MDM](/plugins/mdm).
