# Extras & Special Features

Movies and TV shows may include bonus content.

## Extra Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Trailers** | Promotional trailers |
| **Behind the Scenes** | Making-of content |
| **Deleted Scenes** | Removed scenes |
| **Featurettes** | Short documentaries |
| **Interviews** | Cast and crew interviews |
| **Shorts** | Short films |
| **Samples** | Preview clips |
| **Theme Videos** | Title sequences and theme footage |

## Viewing Extras

### Apple TV

On a movie or show's page, Extras has a category poster beside its gallery.
The poster names the category and shows its item count and running time.

| Action | Control |
|--------|---------|
| Change category | Up / Down while the poster is focused |
| Enter the gallery | Right from the poster |
| Play an extra | Select its card |
| Open the category's page | Select the poster |
| Return from a card to the poster | Menu / Back |

With one category, Up/Down leaves the section normally.
Back preserves the extra you were browsing, and Right returns to it.

The category page has its own Play button, **›** to browse extras, and category tabs below.
Left from Play browses backward; **›** browses forward.
Selecting a card plays that extra.
Playback is one extra at a time.
Back returns to the category poster on the movie or show page.

### iPhone and iPad

Scroll to Extras and tap an extra to play it.
The touch layouts keep their horizontal extras gallery.

Extras supplied by your server play in Neptune's player like other library videos.

## Trailers

The **Trailer** button in the action pill plays the best available trailer:

1. **Local trailer files.**
   When your media library exposes trailer files as special features (for example, `MovieName/Trailers/Official Trailer.mp4` on Jellyfin), they play right in Neptune.
2. **Online trailer.**
   Where your trailer settings allow it, Neptune finds a trailer through your server's metadata or TMDB.
   It tries to play it inside Neptune, with the YouTube app as a fallback when needed.

When an item has several trailers, a **Choose Trailer** picker lists the local ones alongside a **Watch on YouTube** option.

The button only appears when at least one source is likely to yield a trailer.

## Collections

On Apple TV, the **Collections** section has a poster beside the collection's titles.
Select a title to open it, or the poster to open the collection.
Up/Down on the poster switches collections when there are several.

On iPhone and iPad, open **Part of Collection** and tap a collection.
See [Collections](/browsing/collections) for the platform-specific pages and controls.

## Similar Items

The **More Like This** section shows related content based on shared genres, themes, and people.
