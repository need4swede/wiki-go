# People

View detailed information about actors, directors, writers, and other crew members.



## Accessing a Person's Page

Select any person's name from the Cast & Crew section on a movie or TV show detail page. You can also find people through the Library's Actor and Director browse categories.



## What You'll See

### Overview

- Profile photo
- Full biography
- Career span (e.g., "1995 - Present")

### Filmography

The person's work is organized into sections:

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Library Movies** | Movies in your library featuring this person |
| **Library Shows** | TV shows in your library featuring this person |
| **Directed** | Movies and shows this person directed |
| **Written** | Movies and shows this person wrote |
| **Also Known For** | Other notable credits not currently in your library |

When you focus on an item, a pill shows the character or role name (e.g., "as Tony Stark" or "Director").

### Career Timeline

A visual timeline showing the decades this person has been active, giving you a sense of the breadth of their career.



## Insights

Select "Learn More" to see additional details sourced from TMDb, including an extended biography and what the person is best known for.



## Navigation

Select any movie or show in the filmography to navigate to its detail page. Items not in your library will open in the Discover view if Jellyseerr is connected, letting you request them.
