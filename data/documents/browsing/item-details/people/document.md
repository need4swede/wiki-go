# People

View detailed information about actors, directors, writers, and other crew members.



## Accessing a Person's Page

- Select any person in the **Cast & Crew** section of a movie or show
- Browse **Person** in the Library tab's Browse by row, an A to Z grid of everyone across your libraries
- Search for them by name, or find them in the **People** search scope



## What You'll See

### Overview

- Portrait photo
- Birth (and where applicable, death) dates
- Full biography
- A **Pin** button to add them to your [Library Shortcuts](/library/shortcuts)

### Career Timeline

A visual strip showing the decades this person has been active, giving you a sense of the breadth of their career.

### From Library

Everything in your library featuring this person, with search, sorting, and role filters: **All**, **Acted**, **Directed**, **Written**. Focusing an item shows the role pill (e.g., "as Tony Stark").

### From Seerr

With Seerr connected, the page also shows the person's wider filmography beyond your library. Selecting one of these opens its Discover page, where you can request it.



## Insights

Select the biography to open person **Insights**: career overview, fun facts, signature roles, frequent collaborators, and what to watch next. Generated by your configured language model. Requires [AI features](/settings/deep-learning).
