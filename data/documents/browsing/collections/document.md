# Collections

Browse box sets, movie franchises, and other groups of titles together.
Collections can contain movies, shows, or a mix supplied by your server.

## What Are Collections

Common examples include Star Wars, Harry Potter, and The Lord of the Rings.
Each title keeps its own watch status and progress.

## Accessing Collections

1. **Home**: open a collection from the Collections row or its See All view
2. **Item details**: browse the title's Collections section on Apple TV, or Part of Collection on iPhone and iPad
3. **Library**: use the Collections filter, with movie, show, and mixed choices
4. **Search**: collections appear as their own result type
5. **Pins**: open a collection you've pinned

**Group Movies into Collections** in **Settings > Home > Library** can fold movies into collections on the Movies page.

## Collections on a Detail Page

On Apple TV, a collection poster sits beside wide cards for its titles.
If the title belongs to several collections, Up/Down on the poster changes which collection is shown.
Right enters the titles, Select on a title opens it, and Select on the poster opens the collection page.

Back from an inline title returns to the poster without resetting your place.
Right returns to the title you were browsing.

On iPhone and iPad, tap a collection in Part of Collection to open its page.

## Collection Detail Page

### Apple TV

The page follows the season-page layout:

- Collection poster, name, item count, and year range
- The title Play will play, with its description
- Play or Resume, a menu pill, and **›** to browse the titles
- **All**, **Unwatched**, **Watched**, and **Sort** above the title row

Left from Play browses backward through the titles; **›** browses forward.
Down enters the filters and title row.
Up from the filter tabs returns to Play.
Selecting a title opens its details, and holding Select opens its actions.

Sort offers the collection's default order, name, release date, ratings, date added, or runtime.

Opened from a movie's Collections poster, Back returns to that poster.
Back while browsing the collection's lower rows first returns to its hero.

### iPhone and iPad

The collection page shows its poster, name, overview, and a grid of its members.
Tap a title to open its normal detail page.
Touch and hold a card for its available actions.

## Theme Songs

On Apple TV with Jellyfin, a collection's theme music can play while its page is open.
Control it in **Settings > Account > Sounds**.

## Collection Backdrops

On Apple TV, the backdrop follows the title you're browsing, using the collection's artwork as a fallback.

## Tips

- Pin a collection to keep it close while you work through a franchise.
- **Settings > Playback > Autoplay > Autoplay Collections** controls automatic continuation through supported collection playback.
