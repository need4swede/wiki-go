# Recommended

Personalized suggestions based on your watch history, favorites, and the directors and actors you enjoy. The row can blend content from your library with requestable titles from Seerr, so you discover both things you already have and things worth adding.



## Where Suggestions Come From

**Your library.** Your server suggests movies based on what you watch and favorite, and finds shows similar to the series in your Next Up queue.

**Seerr (optional).** When Seerr is connected and **Include Seerr in Recommendations** is on in **Settings > Home > Sections**, popular titles you don't have yet are mixed in. Titles already in your library, already requested, or already tracked by Radarr and Sonarr are filtered out.

Library and requestable items are interleaved evenly, with duplicates removed.



## Badges

Cards carry a small status badge so you can tell at a glance what you have:

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Green checkmark | Already in your library. Opens the detail page. |
| Blue plus | Available to request. Opens the request page. |



## Tab Visibility

| Tab | What's Shown |
|-----|-------------|
| **Home** | All recommendations: movies and shows together |
| **Movies** | Only movie recommendations |
| **Shows** | Only TV show recommendations |



## When Things Aren't Working

| Scenario | Behavior |
|----------|----------|
| Seerr not connected | Shows only library recommendations |
| No watch history yet | Shows popular Seerr titles if connected, otherwise the row hides |
| Everything already in your library | Shows library recommendations only |

Row length is configurable in **Settings > Home > Sections**.
