# Spotlight

A full-bleed backdrop carousel showing content to watch next. The spotlight ranks items from your library based on viewing activity, release dates, and engagement, so the most relevant items appear first.



## Spotlight Categories

Each spotlight item is tagged with a colored badge explaining why it's featured:

| Badge | Color | Criteria |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Continue** | Orange | Movies or episodes you started but didn't finish |
| **New Episode** | Purple | A new episode from a show you're watching, aired within the last week |
| **New Season** | Cyan | A new season of a show you've watched before, premiered within the last two weeks |
| **New Series** | Teal | A brand new show added to your library |
| **New Release** | Blue | A movie that just became available for home viewing |
| **Recently Added** | Green | Fallback. Latest additions when nothing else qualifies. |



## How Items Are Ranked

New episodes and seasons of shows you actively watch rank highest, followed by new releases, then items you're partway through, then recent additions. Within each category, newer beats older, and shows you watched more recently beat ones you haven't touched in a while.

A few refinements keep the queue sensible:

- If several items from the same show qualify, only the strongest one appears. One show never takes up multiple slots.
- Episodes from ended series or older seasons rank lower than the latest season of a current show.
- Movies rank by their **home release date** (when they hit streaming or digital), not their theater date. A movie that ran in theaters three months ago but just became watchable at home counts as new. Home release dates come from TMDB.



## Navigation

- **Left/Right swipe:** Browse between spotlight items
- **Select:** Opens a fullscreen gallery of the spotlight queue; select again to view details
- **Auto-rotation:** Items rotate on their own; the timer resets when you navigate manually

Navigation arrows appear on the left and right edges when the spotlight zone is focused.

Prefer to skip the gallery? Turn on **Direct Navigation** in **Settings > Home > Spotlight** and selecting a spotlight item goes straight to its detail page.



## What Each Item Shows

- The category badge
- The show or movie logo (falls back to a text title if no logo is available)
- A metadata row with genre, season and episode info, air date, rating, and community score
- Media info pills showing resolution, HDR format, audio format, and file size
- Position dots indicating where you are in the carousel



## Per-Tab Queues

Each content tab builds its own spotlight:

| Tab | What's Included |
|-----|-----------------|
| **Home** | All categories: movies, episodes, series, continue watching |
| **Movies** | Movies only |
| **Shows** | Episodes, seasons, and series only |
| **Music** | Music videos only |



## Settings

**Settings > Home > Spotlight** controls:

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Spotlight Limit** | How many items the carousel holds (4 to 12, default 8) |
| **Direct Navigation** | Skip the gallery and open items directly |

Spotlight visibility is shared across the content tabs. Hiding Hero Spotlight in Home customization also hides it on Music; Music's own editor can hide it from Music alone.
