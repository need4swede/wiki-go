# iPhone & iPad

Neptune uses the same library, profile, playback engine, and preferences across
supported clients. On iPhone and iPad, its touch interface also connects
Neptune to iOS-owned surfaces such as Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen widgets,
and Live Activities, and it can continue video or audio after you leave the
app.

## Three Different Shortcut Systems

The word “shortcut” covers three intentionally separate features:

| Feature | Devices | What It Stores | Where You Configure It |
|---------|---------|----------------|------------------------|
| **Native Pins** | Supported Neptune clients | Bookmarks for media, people, and library browse contexts | Pin or unpin the item where it appears in Neptune |
| **Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets** | iPhone and iPad | One Page, Section, or native Pin selection per widget instance | Add the widget in iOS, then choose **Edit Widget** |
| **Compass Shortcuts** | iPhone | One to eight quick actions shared by the in-app Compass and Compass Live Activity | **Settings > User Preferences > Compass > Compass Shortcuts** |

Native Pins are not the same list as Compass Shortcuts. A conventional widget
also does not mirror the Compass: every widget instance has its own selection.



## Availability

- Native Pins and conventional widgets work on both iPhone and iPad.
- Home Screen widgets support the app’s iOS/iPadOS 16 floor. Neptune’s Lock
  Screen widget families require iOS 16 on iPhone and iPadOS 17 on iPad.
- The Compass palette and its Live Activity are iPhone-only.
- Live Activities require iOS 16.1 or later. Dynamic Island presentation also
  requires an iPhone with Dynamic Island; other supported iPhones can still
  show the activity on the Lock Screen.
- Picture in Picture and Background Playback work on iPhone and iPad. Both are
  on by default, can be controlled independently under **Settings >
  Playback**, and apply to the next playback session.
- Backdrop framing and hold-to-reposition are iPhone and iPad features. Apple TV
  keeps filling the screen, because a television is already the shape backdrop
  artwork is made for.
- Activity has the same Overview, History, Insights, Rewind, privacy, and sync
  contract as Apple TV. iPhone and iPad additionally provide touch search and
  locally rendered Rewind share cards.
- Neptune for iPhone and iPad is currently distributed through TestFlight.
  See [Installation](/getting-started/installation).
