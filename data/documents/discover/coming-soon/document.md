# Coming Soon

Coming Soon shows what is arriving this week and what is coming after, using Seerr's release data and your own shows.
Use it to see new episodes of shows you watch, new seasons, finales, and theatrical releases without mixing dates into popularity-ranked rows.

Coming Soon in Discover is not the [Coming Soon row on Home](/browsing/home-screen/coming-soon).
The Home row follows requests and your Radarr and Sonarr calendars.
This page looks at everything about to release.

## Opening Coming Soon

On Apple TV, select the **Coming Soon** calendar icon in Discover's header.
On iPhone and iPad, select **See All** on the **On the Way** row in Discover Today to open **Requests & Coming Soon**.

## Apple TV

A row of filters sits at the top:

| Filter | What It Shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Everything** | Every release |
| **From My Shows** | New episodes and seasons of shows in your library |
| **Movies** | Upcoming movies |
| **Shows** | Upcoming shows and seasons |

Below it is a seven-day ribbon with today's date highlighted.
Each card has a tag:

| Tag | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| **New Episode** | A new episode of a show you have |
| **New Season** | A new season of a show you have |
| **Finale** | A season finale |
| **New Show** | A show premiering for the first time |
| **In Theaters** | A movie's theatrical release |

Releases further out appear under **Later this season**.

Focus a card to open a detail panel on the right.
It tells you what is happening, such as "Season 3 premieres Thursday", and offers one of these:

- **Request**, for a title you don't have
- **Request Season 3**, for a new season of a show you have
- **Go to Show**, to open the show you already own

Each of these opens the title's page, where you finish the request.

## iPhone and iPad

**Requests & Coming Soon** has a chip at the top to jump between Today, Requests, and Coming Soon.
Coming Soon lists up to 40 releases grouped by day: **Today**, then weekdays through the week, then dates.
Turn on **From My Shows** to narrow the list to shows you have.

Each row opens its title and has a **Request** button, or **Request Season N** for a new season.
Your own requests appear above it in the same page, as described in [Requests](/discover/requests#requests-and-coming-soon-on-iphone-and-ipad).

## Empty Weeks

If nothing is releasing, Neptune shows **Nothing this week**.
This does not mean your request history was erased.

## Coming Soon on Home vs. Discover

| View | Source | Purpose |
|------|--------|---------|
| **Discover > Coming Soon** | Seerr's upcoming releases and your shows | Explore what is arriving and request it |
| [Coming Soon on Home](/browsing/home-screen/coming-soon) | Your requests and tracked automation | Follow content already on its way to your server |
