# Browsing Discover

Discover offers several paths into Seerr's wider movie and television catalog.
Each path begins with a visual card and opens a full-screen page that continues loading results as you move through it.

## Explore

**Explore** gathers every path in one place.
On Apple TV, select the **Explore** icon in Discover's header.
On iPhone and iPad, scroll to the **Explore** section of Discover Today.

| Group | What It Holds |
|-------|---------------|
| **Around the World** | Languages, each named in its own script |
| **Decades** | One card per decade |
| **Themes** | Keyword themes, with a card to add your own |
| **Genres** | Movie genres and show genres |
| **Studios and Networks** | Company cards |

On iPhone and iPad, Themes also offers **Anime** and **Search by Keyword**.

## Browse Paths

| Path | Starting Point | Media Choice |
|------|----------------|--------------|
| **Genre** | Explore > Genres | Movie genres or show genres |
| **Era** | Explore > Decades | Movies or TV Shows |
| **Studio** | Explore > Studios | Opens the company's [page](/library/studio-network-pages) |
| **Network** | Explore > Networks | Opens the company's [page](/library/studio-network-pages) |
| **Anime** | The New Anime row's **See All**, or Explore | Movies or TV Shows |
| **Language** | Explore > Around the World | Movies or TV Shows |
| **Keyword** | Explore > Themes | Movies or TV Shows |

## The Browse Page

Genres, decades, languages, themes, and anime open the same kind of page.
It begins with a header for what you chose: the language in its own script, the decade's year, the theme, or the company's name.
Below it:

- **Movies** and **Shows** tabs
- A sort: **Popular**, **Top Rated**, **Newest**, or **Oldest**
- A grid of titles that loads more as you scroll

On iPhone and iPad, the header also says how many of the titles are in your library.
You can search within the titles loaded, sort by **Featured**, and filter by genre, years, and minimum rating.

Titles you own show **In Library**, and titles you've requested show **Requested**.

## Browse by Era

**Decades** in Explore lists each decade.
Choose a decade to explore titles released during that period.
The page opens with separate Movies and TV Shows tabs and preserves the decade while you switch between them.

This is useful for broad exploration—1970s thrillers, 1990s television, or films from the 2020s—without needing a title or genre in mind.

## Genres

Movie genres and TV genres are separate groups because each catalog has its own genre set.
Selecting a card opens a page for that genre, such as Horror Movies or Drama Shows.

## Studios and Networks

Studio cards open pages for companies such as Disney, Warner Bros., Universal, Paramount, Pixar, Marvel Studios, DreamWorks, A24, and others.

Network cards open pages for services and broadcasters such as Netflix, HBO, Apple TV+, Disney+, Amazon, Hulu, BBC One, AMC, and the major US networks.

A company page shows what you own and what you could request.
See [Studio and Network Pages](/library/studio-network-pages).

These are discovery groupings, not a statement about where a title is currently available to stream.

## Anime

The **New Anime** row on Discover shows this season's most popular anime, from AniList.
**See All** opens a dedicated Movies/TV browse.
Neptune checks both sides initially and chooses a useful starting tab when only one contains results.
You can switch types at any time.

## Around the World

Around the World browses by a title's original language rather than its country of production or current audio tracks.
It includes more than twenty languages, including Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, French, Arabic, and Portuguese.

Selecting a language opens a Movies/TV grid scoped to that language.
This is separate from the global [Language Preferences](/discover#language-preferences) filter.

## Themed Collections

Themed Collections use catalog keywords instead of Neptune's on-device semantic tags.
A theme such as Time Travel or Based on a True Story opens a Movies/TV browse across Seerr's catalog, including titles outside your library.

See [Themed Collections](/discover/themed-collections) to customize the row.

## Infinite Browsing

Browse pages load an initial grid, then fetch the next page automatically when you reach the end.
New items append without replacing what is already visible, and duplicates are removed between pages.

If the first page fails, an error state appears.
A later pagination failure leaves the results you already loaded in place.

## Selecting an Item

Every grid uses the same availability-aware detail routing as the main Discover rows:

- Available media opens its playable library detail page.
- Partially available shows open with owned seasons and request options for the rest.
- Unavailable media opens a requestable detail page.

See [Requests](/discover/requests) for what happens after you select **Request**.
