# Ask in Discover

Ask finds titles you don't have yet.
Say what you're in the mood for, such as "a cozy mystery for a rainy night" or "sci-fi like Arrival", and Neptune AI suggests titles with a line on why each one fits.
You can request a pick without leaving the page.

Ask is separate from [Search](/search).
Search looks through your library.
Ask looks for new things to request.

**Requirements:** Seerr connected, language models enabled, and [Neptune Pro](/neptune-pro/neptune-ai).
Ask works the same on every media server.

## Opening Ask

| Device | How |
|--------|-----|
| **iPhone and iPad** | Open **Discover > Today** and type in the **Ask** field |
| **Apple TV** | Select the **Search** icon in Discover's header. The keyboard opens right away |

Without Neptune Pro, Ask is marked **PRO** and opens the upgrade screen.
Ask is hidden for child accounts and accounts with parental limits.

## What You Get

Each suggestion is a real title, matched to its TMDB entry through Seerr, so Ask doesn't show titles that don't exist.
Anything already in your library is left out.
At the bottom, Neptune says how many suggestions you already had.
Titles you have requested stay in the list and show **Requested**.

On iPhone and iPad, the page shows your question, Neptune AI's reading of it, and one large card per pick with its backdrop, year, a reason, and **Request**.
Select **Request** to request it in place.

On Apple TV, Ask is one screen.
The focused pick fills the background with its logo, year, and why it fits.
**New Question** and **Refine** sit above a row of posters.
Move along the row to change the pick, and select a poster to open its page, where you can request it.

While Neptune AI works, you see your question with a loading animation.
If it can't find anything new, it says so and suggests asking another way.
If it can't be reached, you can try again.

## Refine

Once the answer is complete, **Refine** asks again with a twist and leaves out the titles you've already seen.

- **Darker**
- **Lighter**
- **Newer**
- **Older**
- **Shorter**
- **Shows Only**
- **Movies Only**

On Apple TV, **New Question** asks something different.

## Privacy

Ask sends your question to Neptune AI.
It also sends the names of up to 12 titles you finished watching recently, with no ids or times, because your taste is what makes the picks good.

Whether you already own a title is checked on your device and with Seerr.
Neptune AI doesn't receive your library.
Answers aren't saved.

## Expectations

Suggestions can be wrong, and Neptune AI can miss very new or obscure titles.
Ask always uses Neptune AI.
It doesn't use a model you connect yourself.
